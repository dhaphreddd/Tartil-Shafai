## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a tranquil, focused digital environment engineered exclusively for Quranic recitation and curated maqra' study. The design aesthetic is grounded in **Refined Minimalist Editorial** with sacred reverence. It deliberately eschews cliché Islamic web decorations—such as heavy geometric arabesques, dome illustrations, saturated green backdrops, and faux-gold gradients—in favor of expansive atmospheric whitespace, flawless typographic rhythm, and an intuitive reading flow.

The target audience spans students, teachers, and daily reciters who require deep focus without visual friction. The emotional posture is meditative, dignified, and serene: entering the interface feels akin to stepping into a quiet, sunlit library or opening an exquisitely typeset physical mus-haf. 

Visual principles emphasize quiet distinction:
- **Calm Surface Hierarchy:** Micro-contrasting warm parchment tones replace stark clinical whites.
- **Content Precedence:** Digital Quranic text takes absolute priority; navigation, toolbars, and search elements recede into the peripheral canvas until invoked.
- **Deliberate Restraint:** Warm gold serves strictly as a focused anchor for active wayfinding and sacred ayah markers, never as ornamental flood.

## Colors

The color palette centers on an organic, paper-inspired foundational spectrum paired with a dignified, non-metallic antique gold. 

### Palette Application
- **Light Theme**: The background `#FAFAF7` evokes warm, unbleached cotton paper. Primary `#9A7738` brings grounded warmth to interactive anchors, active tabs, and verse delimiters without fatiguing the eye. Surface cards use `#FFFFFF` bordered with `#E7E3D9` to achieve subtle physical boundaries without drop shadows.
- **Dark Theme**: The night palette utilizes a deep charcoal foundation `#111111` rather than pure `#000000`, eliminating harsh halation during nocturnal recitation. The primary shifts up to `#C6A15B` for high-legibility luminance against `#181818` card containers.
- **Accent Philosophy**: Warm muted gold is an informational guide, not a decorative fill. It is restricted to active tab underlines, primary search triggers, current ayah indicators, and package progression badges.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is split cleanly into two domains: the utilitarian Latin UI shell (`Inter`) and the sacred Quranic reading plane (`Noto Naskh Arabic`).

### Arabic Reading Principles
- **Atmospheric Line Heights:** Arabic glyphs with complex vocalization (tashkeel/harakat) require vertical breathing space. Line-height is strictly scaled between `2.2` and `2.4` times the font size to prevent overlapping diacritics.
- **Continuous Reading Flow:** Verses are presented as continuous, centered or carefully justified prose streams rather than isolated conversational cards.
- **Ayah End Delimiters:** Aya markers (`۝` and Arabic numerals) are embedded inline within the text flow at `0.85em` relative size in the primary accent hue, maintaining baseline harmony without causing line hops.
- **Scalability:** The user reading controls (`Aa-`, `Aa+`) increment the base font size by discrete steps (`22px` -> `26px` -> `30px` -> `34px` -> `38px`), with line heights recalculating dynamically.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy balances dense utilitarian navigation with an airy, distraction-free reading canvas.

### Reading Column Constraint
- **Reader Containment:** On desktop views, reading streams never expand past a fixed maximum reading measure (`720px` to `780px`). This mirrors standard codex ergonomics, preserving optimal horizontal scanning speed.
- **Public & Home Layouts:** Utilize a centered 12-column layout (max width `1120px`), allowing home cards, search fields, and volume selectors to anchor comfortably with broad side margins.

### Breakpoint Matrix
- **Mobile (`320px – 767px`):** Single column stack. Margin is `1rem (16px)`. Bottom toolbar replaces top floating widgets. Sidebar navigation becomes a full slide-over or compact bottom drawer.
- **Tablet (`768px – 1023px`):** Margins expand to `2rem (32px)`. Dual-column displays for package listings; drawer-based navigation for Quran reading mode.
- **Desktop (`1024px+`):** Persistent or collapsible auxiliary reading index (`280px` wide) docked to the left of the centered `760px` reading viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies entirely on **low-contrast outlines and micro-tonal surface layering** rather than heavy ambient or cast shadows. 

- **Surface Levels (Light Mode):** 
  - `Base Canvas`: `#FAFAF7`
  - `Raised Surface (Cards, Navbars, Sheets)`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `Inset / Hover / Subtle`: `#F4F2EC`
- **Surface Levels (Dark Mode):** 
  - `Base Canvas`: `#111111`
  - `Raised Surface (Cards, Menus)`: `#181818`
  - `Elevated Surface (Modals, Overlays)`: `#202020`
- **Ghost Borders:** Elements are defined by a crisp `1px` structural border (`#E7E3D9` light, `#2A2A2A` dark). 
- **Shadow Elimination:** No standard drop shadows are used on cards, inputs, or rows. Modals, search dropdown overlays, and bottom sheets use a soft, non-directional shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)` in light mode, and `0 8px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)` in dark mode to separate overlays from the text background.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric form language. Softened corners introduce contemporary warmth while maintaining the disciplined structure of a scholarly text catalog.

- **Buttons & Search Inputs:** `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) radius, conveying intentional, quiet precision.
- **Cards & Reading Frames:** `0.5rem` (8px) border radius for subtle structure without bubble-like distraction.
- **Filter Chips & Numerical Anchors:** Rounded pills (`9999px`) are reserved solely for numeric package indicators (`01`, `02`), ayah tokens, and category tags to differentiate interactive metadata from text blocks.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button**: Solid fill with Light `#9A7738` / Dark `#C6A15B`, displaying white or near-black text (`#111111`). Padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`, height `42px` (desktop), `46px` (mobile touch target). Border radius `6px`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: `1px` border using `#E7E3D9` (dark: `#2A2A2A`) with surface background. Hover states tint subtly to `#F4F2EC` (dark: `#202020`).
- **Icon Controls**: Square `44x44px` minimum touch footprint, transparent background, rendering `#6F6F6F` icons that shift to primary gold on hover or active states.

### Search Input
- Clean, prominent input field. Height `48px` (desktop) / `52px` (mobile). Background `#FFFFFF` (dark: `#181818`), border `1px solid #E7E3D9` (dark: `#2A2A2A`). 
- Focus state switches border to primary gold with zero default browser outline blur. Includes inline trailing clear trigger and leading search glyph.

### Package List Rows (Compact & Mobile Cards)
- **Desktop**: Compact linear table row, height `54px`. Column 1: Monospaced gold index (`01`, `02`). Column 2: Surah title in semibold `15px`. Column 3: Ayah range in secondary text. Column 4: Subtle chevron. Hover renders background `#F4F2EC` across the row.
- **Mobile**: Light-bordered card container (`border: 1px solid var(--border)`). Number badge docked top-left, surah name prominent, ayah span clearly labeled below with rightward chevron.

### Volume (Jilid) Selection Cards
- Clean editorial containers. Large volume designation (`Jilid 1`), package count badge (`40 Paket`), and arrow link. Zero complex illustration; clean typographic hierarchy.

### Reading Surface & Ayah Blocks
- Centered container (`max-width: 760px`).
- Bismillah header centered with generous top/bottom spacing (`3rem`).
- Continuous verse formatting with RTL script direction.
- Ayah delimiters: Circular or ornate brackets holding the ayah number in gold typography.

### Reader Floating Action Bar & Bottom Sheet
- Mobile Reader bottom bar: Docked floating navigation anchored `16px` above the bottom screen safe area, enclosed in `#FFFFFF`/`#181818` with subtle border and low ambient shadow. Features quick font adjustment (`Aa`), dark mode toggle, and surah index.
- Settings sheet: Slides from bottom on mobile; lists text size, line height steps, and theme selection as discrete segmented buttons.
